    Questions

    Before you buy

    How do I pay?

    Select your ticket and press Buy Now. The payment instructions and exact amount will then appear at checkout.

    When do I get my ticket?

    After you submit your name, phone, email and M-Pesa code, we check the payment against our paybill records. Once it matches, your ticket is emailed to you.

    Where do I find the M-Pesa confirmation code?

    It is the 10 letters and numbers at the start of the SMS M-Pesa sends after you pay.

    What do the couple and table tickets cover?

    The Golden Circle couple ticket admits 2 people for one combo price. The Golden Circle table ticket admits a group of 8 for one combo price.
